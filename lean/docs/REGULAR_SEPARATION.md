# Positive-excess regular cross-transversality

## Main source-level endpoint

`UploadBroadcast.RegularPositiveCross.cross_disjoint` proves, for an actual
finite-dimensional three-flag task over **any field**,

- simultaneous indecomposability of its six actual subspaces;
- zero directed defect;
- positive side excess, `finrank F < finrank A₀ + finrank A₁ + finrank A₂`;

imply `Disjoint (Uᵢ) (Aⱼ)` for every `i ≠ j`.

The theorem does not assume a real-root equation, a dimension family, equality
of private-space dimensions, an invertible matching, a graph normal form, or a
tube classification. It does not need a side-spanning hypothesis. The stronger
endpoint `positive_regular_generated_by_pair` constructs an actual finite
extension certificate using the two concrete regular-simple objects T₀ and T₁.

This is one structural theorem. Passing its build and axiom audit is not by
itself a claim that every theorem in the manuscript has been verified.

## Proof spine

1. **Semistability from actual Hom vanishing.**
   `RegularSubobjectDefect` establishes that no positive-defect task injects
   into a zero-defect indecomposable. `SemistableFiltration` applies this to
   every induced actual ambient subspace. Its predicate `Task.IsSemistable`
   requires zero total defect and nonpositive defect on every induced subtask.

2. **Actual finite regular-simple filtration.**
   The quotient `Task.quotientBy P` uses the images of all six designated
   subspaces. Exact rank-nullity proves additive defect on every quotient
   subspace and its preimage. Zero-defect induced subobjects and quotients stay
   semistable. Minimizing positive ambient rank gives a regular-simple actual
   subobject. Strict ambient-rank induction gives `RegularFiltration`.

3. **Strict morphisms and simple orthogonality.**
   `SemistableMorphisms` applies six rank-nullity equations to a preserving
   ambient map. The kernel and induced image have defect zero, and the image
   of each designated source space equals the target-space intersection with
   the full ambient image. A nonzero map between regular-simple tasks is
   consequently an actual configuration isomorphism.

4. **Concrete two-object Hom–Euler identities.**
   `RegularTripodPair` uses T₀, the passive three-line tripod in a plane, and
   T₁, the one-dimensional task with all sides zero and all totals equal to
   the ambient line. It proves their regular simplicity and four Hom–Euler
   identities by actual kernel and annihilator equivalences. Every other
   regular-simple class is Hom-orthogonal to both, with zero directed Euler
   pairing in both directions.

5. **Constructive extension separation.**
   `QuiverExtensions` identifies the actual morphism constraints with the
   kernel of a linear coboundary map. Equality of Hom dimension and Euler
   pairing makes this coboundary surjective. `TaskExactSequence` proves
   pointwise exactness of every induced-task/quotient sequence.
   `QuiverExactSequence` presents it as an actual block extension, and
   `RegularExtensionGeneration` converts the filtration into an
   `ExtensionGenerated` certificate.

   `CoboundaryExtensionClosure` proves obstruction vanishing is closed under
   extensions in either variable and under actual isomorphisms.
   `ExtensionBlockSeparation` explicitly removes the two cross-class arrow
   blocks by linear shears. Thus an indecomposable generated by two mutually
   extension-orthogonal classes belongs entirely to one class.
   `ExtensionHomVanishing` propagates Hom orthogonality through the same
   finite extension certificates.

6. **Detection and the required intersections.**
   Positive side excess gives a nonzero actual map T₀ → T, by the explicit
   relation-space formula for Hom(T₀,T). It rules out the outside class.
   `CrossIntersectionClosure` proves injectivity of the natural two-path map
   `R.middle i × R.outer j → R.center` is extension-closed and invariant under
   actual isomorphism. Both T₀ and T₁ satisfy it for distinct arms. For an
   actual task, this injectivity is exactly `Disjoint (Uᵢ) (Aⱼ)`.

## Supplementary regular-pencil helper

`GradedRelationSplit` independently proves that a positive idempotent power
of `T³ − 1` has a supported two-sided inverse for `1 − T`. Grades preserved
by `T³` are preserved by the projector, and arbitrary coupling rows factor
on its image. Finiteness is used to find the idempotent power. This helper is
**not required** in the proof spine above and does not claim that an arbitrary
pencil has an invertible matching.

## Checks

The modules were compiled with the pinned Lean/mathlib toolchain. The scoped
transitive audit is `verification/GradedSeparationAudit.lean`, with output in
`logs/graded-separation-axioms.log`. It permits only `propext`,
`Classical.choice`, and `Quot.sound`. The main theorem's downstream finite-field
Jordan classification is handled separately; its additional hypotheses should
not be confused with those of `RegularPositiveCross.cross_disjoint`.
